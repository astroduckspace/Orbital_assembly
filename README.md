# Orbital_assembly
orbital assembly (moduducks V5.0) is a fully modular space station construction system 
orbital assembly features 49 complete, fully compatable modules
<img width="2048" height="1536" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-29 at 13 39 59" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ada643f0-016b-4551-b42f-0b8f8ca714dc" />

these include:

Two straight modules

Four curved modules 

Six T junctions

Two X junctions

Four Vertical X junctions

all truss connector configurations (Straight, curved, I/T/X junctions, ect)

three solar modules (Small, Medium, Large)

Two radiator modules (2 and 4)

two RTG modules

Sixteen cap modules (solar variants, windows, ect)

notes:
Orbital assembly Does not require any external equipment over than a 3d printer and some glue for the solar pannels
DO NOT APPLY TOO MUCH PRESSURE TO CONNECTORS- while if printed properly these connectors should attach without forcing it, DO NOT APPLY HEAVY PRESSURE it often causes the clips to snap
DO NOT ATTEMPT TO REGLUE CLIPS IF BROKEN, if the clips on a module have broken, don't try to fix them they will just snap again.
